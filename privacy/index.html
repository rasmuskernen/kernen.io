Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

What this covers

This policy applies to Maestro, an internal assistant tool operated by Kernen Digital AB (Örebro, Sweden) for Rasmus Isak Kernen. Maestro connects to Google accounts owned by Rasmus Isak Kernen and the companies he works in. It is not offered to the public, and no other users can sign in to it.

Data accessed

With the account owner's consent, Maestro may access Google user data such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Search Console and Google Analytics, limited to the permissions granted on the consent screen.

How data is used

  • Data is used only to help the account owner read, summarise and organise their own information, and to prepare drafts and documents they have asked for.
  • Nothing is sent, published, shared or deleted without the account owner's explicit approval.
  • Data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties.
  • Data is not used to train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored locally on the account owner's own computer and are never committed to shared storage. Data is processed only for the task at hand and is not kept by Maestro beyond that.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Maestro's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
